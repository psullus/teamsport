Help

Learn how to use the platform and get the most out of its features.

Getting Started

To start using the platform, create an account on the sign up page. You'll need to provide your organisation name, email address, and a password. After signing up, a verification email will be sent to your inbox — click the link in that email to verify your account. Once verified, you can log in and access all features.

Your Profile

View your account information on the profile page, including your name, email, phone number, organisation, and role. You can update your first name, last name, phone number, and avatar from the profile page. You can upload a profile picture that will appear in place of your default initials.

Organisations

An organisation is automatically created for you when you sign up. It represents your club, league, or group and serves as the top-level container for all your teams, clubs, and members. The name you provide during registration becomes your organisation name.

Clubs & Teams

Clubs and teams help you organise members within your organisation. Admins can create clubs and teams, assign members to them, and manage their rosters. Each club can contain multiple teams, and members can belong to more than one team. Use clubs to group related teams together (e.g. age groups, divisions).

Leagues

Leagues let you run competitions between clubs or teams. Admins can create a league, add participants, and schedule fixtures manually or generate a full round-robin schedule automatically. Each fixture tracks scores, status (scheduled, completed, or no-show), and individual scorers. The platform automatically calculates standings (points, wins, draws, losses, goal difference) and a top scorers table. Leagues can be archived when a season ends and unarchived later if needed.

Events

Events let you share upcoming activities with your organisation — training sessions, matches, social gatherings, or anything else. Each event has a title, date, host, and optional details like start/end times, location, contacts, and a description. Events can also be marked as all-day. The home page splits events into upcoming and past tabs so members can quickly see what's next.

Roles & Permissions

USER
The default role assigned to new members. Users can view their own profile, but cannot manage other members or access the account settings.
ADMIN
Organisation administrators can access the account settings to manage members within their organisation, invite new users, change roles, and manage clubs, teams, leagues, and events.
