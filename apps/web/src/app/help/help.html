Help

Learn how to use the platform and get the most out of its features.

Getting Started

To start using the platform, create an account on the sign up page. You'll need to provide your organisation name, email address, and a password. After signing up, a verification email will be sent to your inbox — click the link in that email to verify your account. Once verified, you can log in and access all features.

Your Profile

View your account information on the profile page, including your name, email, phone number, organisation, and role. To update your details — such as your first name, last name, phone number, or avatar — visit the settings page. You can upload a profile picture that will appear in place of your default initials.

Organisations

An organisation is automatically created for you when you sign up. It represents your club, league, or group and serves as the top-level container for all your teams, clubs, and members. The name you provide during registration becomes your organisation name.

Clubs & Teams

Clubs and teams help you organise members within your organisation. Admins can create clubs and teams, assign members to them, and manage their rosters. Each club can contain multiple teams, and members can belong to more than one team. Use clubs to group related teams together (e.g. age groups, divisions).

Roles & Permissions

USER
The default role assigned to new members. Users can view their own profile and settings, but cannot manage other members or access the admin dashboard.
ADMIN
Organisation administrators can access the admin dashboard to manage members within their organisation, invite new users, change roles, and manage clubs and teams.
CONTROL
System-level administrators with full access across all organisations. Control users can access the control panel for platform-wide management in addition to all admin capabilities.

Admin Dashboard

The admin dashboard is available to users with the ADMIN or CONTROL role. From here you can view all members in your organisation, invite new members by email, change a member's role, and remove members from the organisation. Access it via the "Organisation dashboard" link in the profile menu.

Control Panel

The control panel is reserved for users with the CONTROL role. It provides system-level management capabilities across all organisations on the platform. Access it via the "Control panel" link in the profile menu.
